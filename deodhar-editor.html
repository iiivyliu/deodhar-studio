Definitions & scope

The default follows Definition 2.1 on page 2 of the Deodhar Diagram and Rational Dyck Path notes.For an A × B rectangle, A and B must be coprime; each pipe must connect matching boundary labels; the bottom-left label of every elbow must be smaller than its top-right label; and there must be exactly (A−1)(B−1) crossings. Equivalently, there are A+B−1 elbows.

Read cells in order. A crossing swaps adjacent positions; an elbow leaves them unchanged and is allowed only when their current labels are increasing. Boundary numbers are fixed target labels. Mismatched exits are marked red; pipe colors follow the actual labels.

A general Deodhar diagram only requires the distinguished condition (Definition 1.1 on page 1 of the notes). Identity and maximality are additional conditions available in the validation rules.

Convention for custom shapes: Cell coordinates increase rightward in x and upward in y. Let d be the minimum x+y over all selected cells. A cell represents s_(x+y−d+1). Read selected cells bottom to top, right to left within each row, to obtain a positive word. The strand count n is one more than its largest generator index. Blank cells are not part of the word and do not count as elbows. Translating the entire shape does not change the checks.

Custom reading order: Click Custom order, number the cells, then apply. Independent operations may be exchanged: their generator indices differ by more than 1. Equal or adjacent indices must retain their original order. This changes the reading sequence without changing the diagram or validity result. Incompatible sequences show the missing prerequisites and must be corrected before applying. Tile changes preserve the applied order; adding or removing cells resets it to the default. Undo restores the previous order. Saving a diagram includes its applied order.

You can edit staircases, missing corners, holes, and disconnected cells. For arbitrary shapes, the checker evaluates a mask on the word above; it does not assume the shape is a Young diagram from the notes. Faint dashed connectors route identity pipes through blank cells. Custom shapes are checked for distinguishedness and identity by default; maximality is not determined.

The optional Mₙ rule also requires exactly n−1 elbows. A passing mask is labeled maximal only when the full word gives a single n-cycle. This is not a general maximality test for arbitrary shapes. Non-coprime rectangles are marked outside the definition under the default rule.

Published definitions: Galashin–Lam, Definitions 9.2–9.3. This tool checks combinatorial conditions; it does not compute a Deodhar-to-Dyck-path bijection.
